How to Build an AI Rubric

  • Dimension: What are you measuring? (e.g., "Conciseness", "Technical Accuracy").
  • Scale: Use a Likert scale (1-5) or binary (Pass/Fail) for automated assertions.
  • Description: Explicitly define what a "5" looks like vs. a "1" to minimize judge variance.
  • Reference: Provide a "Golden Answer" as a benchmark for the LLM-as-a-Judge to compare against.
